# Skool Video Downloader - Product Mission & Rules

## Product Overview
**Name:** Skool Video Downloader
**Keywords:** `skool video downloader`, `skool video saver`
**Domain:** http://skoolvideosaver.com/
**Purpose:** A Chrome extension that allows users to save videos from Skool classroom lessons, community posts, and course replays as local MP4 files.

## Core Value Propositions
1. **Never Lose Access:** Save content before memberships or cohorts expire.
2. **Privacy-First:** 100% local processing in the browser; no external servers.
3. **Universal Detection:** Automatically handles Skool native (Mux), Loom, Vimeo, Wistia, and YouTube.
4. **Offline Learning:** Study anywhere without an internet connection.
