# Skool Video Downloader

The #1 Chrome extension for saving videos from Skool classroom lessons, community posts, and course replays as local MP4 files.

## Features
- **Native Mux Support:** Save Skool's native video player streams.
- **Universal Detection:** Works with Loom, Vimeo, Wistia, and YouTube embeds.
- **Local Processing:** 100% private, no data leaves your browser.
- **High Quality:** Save in original resolution up to 4K.
