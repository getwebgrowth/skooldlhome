# Skool Video Downloader

The Chrome extension for saving videos from Skool classroom lessons, community posts, and course replays as local MP4 files.

## Features
- **Native Mux Support:** Save Skool's native video player streams.
- **Embedded Players:** Works with Loom, Vimeo, and Wistia embeds inside Skool.
- **Local Processing:** Videos are downloaded and converted in your browser, never on our servers.
