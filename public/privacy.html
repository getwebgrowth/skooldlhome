Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 05, 2026

At Skool Video Downloader, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our Chrome extension.

No Data Tracking

We do not track your browsing history, your Skool login credentials, or the content of the videos you download. All video detection and processing happens locally on your own computer.

Personal Information

We only collect personal information (such as your email address) when you voluntarily provide it to us, for example, when purchasing a Pro license or contacting support.

Third-Party Services

We use Stripe for payment processing. Your credit card information is sent directly to Stripe and never touches our servers. We also use basic analytics to understand how many people visit our website, but this does not include any identifying information from the extension itself.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@skoolvideosaver.com.
